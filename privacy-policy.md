# Privacy Policy — Business Platform

**Last Updated: 22 October 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Business Platform is a software platform being developed to help businesses manage customer communications through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when using Business Platform.

For privacy-related questions, contact:

**Email:** gabrielsam2k@gmail.com

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, Business Platform may process:

- Business account information, including business names, contact details, and account settings.
- User information, including names, email addresses, and login details.
- Customer contact information uploaded by businesses, including names and telephone numbers.
- WhatsApp message information, campaign records, message identifiers, and delivery statuses.
- Technical and security information necessary to operate and protect the service.

Businesses using the platform are responsible for ensuring they have an appropriate legal basis and any required consent to upload contacts and send WhatsApp communications.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide and maintain Business Platform services.
- Connect business accounts with the WhatsApp Business Platform.
- Process authorized WhatsApp messages and campaigns.
- Display message delivery statuses and campaign reports.
- Manage accounts, trials, subscriptions, and customer support.
- Maintain platform security and investigate misuse.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Business Platform does not authorize unsolicited or unlawful bulk messaging.

## 4. WhatsApp and Meta Services

Business Platform is designed to integrate with the official WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta.

When a business connects its WhatsApp account, relevant information may be exchanged with Meta to send messages, receive webhook notifications, and provide messaging functionality.

Use of WhatsApp messaging is also subject to Meta's applicable terms, policies, pricing, and messaging limits.

Business Platform is an independent software service and is not presented as being owned or operated by Meta.

## 5. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate the platform, including hosting providers and Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to address security incidents.

Business Platform does not intend to sell customer contact lists or WhatsApp message information to advertisers or data brokers.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

Business Platform aims to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Data storage locations, service providers, and security practices may change as the platform develops. This Privacy Policy should be updated to reflect material changes.

## 7. Data Retention

Account information, contact records, campaign records, and message statuses may be retained while needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the platform.

Retention periods may vary by data type and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, reasonable steps will be taken to delete or anonymize it, subject to legitimate retention obligations.

## 9. Account Trials and Subscriptions

Business Platform may offer free trials, paid subscriptions, and selected complimentary accounts.

Subscription arrangements do not automatically include Meta's WhatsApp messaging charges. Applicable Meta charges may be payable separately under the customer's WhatsApp billing arrangement.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Business Platform is intended for business use and is not directed at children.

If personal information relating to a child is identified as having been processed improperly, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.
